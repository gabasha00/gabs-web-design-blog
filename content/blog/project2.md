Our project focuses on mapping real-time environmental data; every time a new wave height or an earthquake magnitude is recorded from the APIs of NOAA and USGS, it is reflected as EKG type lines on the website. The Earth is always moving, “breathing,” and “pulsing,” and we see this as seismic shifts are always causing wave level disruption and earthquakes. Representing this through EKG lines reflects this idea, and we aimed to use that to make it as dynamic as possible.
