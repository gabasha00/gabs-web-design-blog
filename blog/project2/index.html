The Earth is Breathing

  • 25 March 2026

Our project focuses on mapping real-time environmental data; every time a new wave height or an earthquake magnitude is recorded from the APIs of NOAA and USGS, it is reflected as EKG type lines on the website. The Earth is always moving, “breathing,” and “pulsing,” and we see this as seismic shifts are always causing wave level disruption and earthquakes. Representing this through EKG lines reflects this idea, and we aimed to use that to make it as dynamic as possible.

In the beginning, I didn't really have much inspiration. I can't speak for Kris and Scott, but I knew for my part of this project I wanted to make sure the website was at the very least fun to look at (even if somebody has no idea what's going on). Kris's blog will entail more of the design process, but creating the backbone / framework was simple. Creating a logo with the Earth as the center and laying out the location options to make sure people know exactly where to go was priority.
