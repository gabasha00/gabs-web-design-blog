  • 27 February 2026

The Earth is Breathing by Scott, Kris and Gabby

Our project aims to visualize the Earth by taking environmental data from APIs such as NOAA and USGS and turning them into kinds of wave signals that behave as if they're beating on a pulse. As Kris states in their blog post about our concept, the ocean will serve as a steady "base," and when an earthquake has occurred, they will appear as sudden disruptions, creating the "pulse effect."

Our current baseline of steps are:

  • Step 1: Using the NOAA data as the base data for the steady ocean wave
  • Step 2: Potentially implementing Scott's idea where the amplitude = waveHeight and the mapping speed are the wavePeriod data
  • Step 3: Implementing the seismic layers for the earthquakes and the pulse disturbances using USGS data (spikes appearing when earthquakes occur)
  • Step 4: Add-ons, subtle color shifts, perhaps adding sound effects to pulses? Deciding about transitions, etc.

Scott shared an example of a visualization created using p5.js with us.
